# ROMI — The Travel Companion

A mobile-first road-life companion for nomads, campers, remote workers, and travelers.

## Mission

Help people quickly find what they need for real life on the road: sleep, water, bathrooms, food/fuel, laundry, dog needs, and workable connectivity—using real place information and honest traveler reports.

## Version 1

- Search real places and explore nearby needs
- Open useful place-detail pages
- Save places to a private account
- Create multiple road plans
- Submit Scout reports and photos
- Admin review before community reports become public
- A bright, simple Today dashboard
